# Evidence: cart, checkout, payments, delivery, returns, account, post-purchase + competitor benchmark

Scope: goldentech.com.sa (Golden Technology / التكنولوجيا الذهبية), plus extra.com, jarir.com, amazon.sa, noon.com, and KSA gaming-PC specialists.
Method: 43 WebSearch (extended) queries, run on 2026-10-22. No direct page fetches were possible.
Caveat: every "quote" below is text that the search summariser reported from the indexed page. These are close to verbatim but were not read off the live page, so re-check the live page before quoting on a slide. Index dates vary, and some pages may have changed since.

Tags: **[verified: quote+URL]** means the summariser quoted the page text and gave the URL. **[inferred]** means my reading or a third-party source. "not found in index" means no search turned it up.

## PART A: Golden Tech (goldentech.com.sa)

### A1. Delivery fees and free shipping (three different answers)

- **[verified: quote+URL]** Terms page sets a flat fee: "Product delivery cost: 20 SAR per order." https://goldentech.com.sa/en/terms-and-conditions
- **[verified: quote+URL]** The same Terms page adds a COD surcharge: "Cost of delivering a product when choosing a pay-on-delivery feature: 20 SAR for pay-on-delivery." https://goldentech.com.sa/en/terms-and-conditions
- **[verified: quote+URL]** The FAQ (EN and AR) uses a threshold model that contradicts the Terms: "If the order needs delivery and its value is less than 200 SAR, thus the delivery fee will be 10 SAR. And if the order value is 200 SAR or more, the delivery will be free of charge." https://goldentech.com.sa/en/faqs and https://goldentech.com.sa/ar/faqs
- **[verified: quote+URL]** Marketing promises free shipping everywhere. The X/Twitter post @GoldenTechSA/status/1852731613991301162 says "🚚شحن مجاني وسريع لكل مدن المملكة" ("free and fast shipping to all cities in the Kingdom"). https://x.com/GoldenTechSA/status/1852731613991301162
- **[verified: quote+URL]** The brand page "Golden Tech" on the store was summarised as advertising free shipping to all cities in KSA. https://goldentech.com.sa/en/shop-by-brand/golden_tech (summariser paraphrase, no exact quote)
- **[inferred]** UX impact: a shopper can find three answers to "what will delivery cost?": 20 SAR flat, 10 SAR under 200 SAR / free above, or free everywhere. Since almost every laptop costs more than 200 SAR, the 20 SAR Terms figure is either stale or a surprise at checkout. Either way it damages trust.

### A2. Cash on delivery (COD)

- **[verified: quote+URL]** Terms: COD is accepted "as an alternative to card payment, with an additional 20 riyals to be charged for this service." https://goldentech.com.sa/en/terms-and-conditions
- **[verified: quote+URL]** The FAQ lists "Cash at exhibitions" (showroom cash) as a payment method and does not mention COD. https://goldentech.com.sa/en/faqs ; the AR FAQ lists "Visa, Mastercard, mada, and cash at the showrooms". https://goldentech.com.sa/ar/faqs
- **[inferred]** The Payment Methods page, Terms and FAQ each list a different set of methods. Benchmark: Amazon.sa charges 17 SAR for COD and Black Box KSA charges 29 SR (see Part B), so 20 SAR is in the normal range. The issue is that it is not shown consistently.
